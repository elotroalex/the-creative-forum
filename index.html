Welcome.

Now more than ever, we are asked to think about our cultures and predicaments through a bevy of disciplines and lenses. The Creative Forum accepts the challenge. Our mission is simple: to bring together the creative arts and scholarship in closer dialogue with one another to help us better understand the worlds we inherit, and inspire us to create new ones. Today, the Forum is home to the creative work of the Department of Spanish & Portuguese: our literary magazine, our film festival, and the projects still to come.

Looking for talks, readings and other events? Visit the Department of Spanish & Portuguese.
